Election Night Review

Unit 3: Political Parties and the Electoral College

How to play. Teams take turns picking a category and value. Read the question aloud, give teams time to agree on an answer, then press Show answer (or the space bar). Click the green check for each team that got it right. Wrong answers cost points only if you click the red X.

One tile is a Daily Double: the team that picks it wagers up to its score (or up to 500 if it has less). Finish with Final round, where every team wagers before seeing the question. Scores save in this browser, so a refresh won’t lose the game.
